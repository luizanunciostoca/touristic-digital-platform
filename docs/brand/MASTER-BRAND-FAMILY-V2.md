The family shares a geometric and behavioral grammar without repeating one symbol across destinations. Shared characteristics include circular nodes, connected curves, round route terminals, optical spacing logic, common typography hierarchy and a coral human/value node.

## Concept 02 authority

The TDP V2 symbol is a controlled vector reconstruction of the owner-approved Concept 02 reference. It restores the approximately spherical network silhouette, asymmetric orbital routes, distributed nodes and blue-to-cyan-to-green system with a coral value node.

The previous tall/atom-like TDP reinterpretation is not equivalent to Concept 02 and is governed as superseded historical material.

## Responsive marks

Each brand has separate full-symbol and micro-symbol artwork. The micro marks deliberately remove geometry and increase route weight for small-size recognition.

The complete institutional package contains:

- full, compact, symbol and micro configurations;
- gradient, flat, black, white and grayscale variants;
- SVG, PNG, WebP and vector PDF outputs;
- app icons from 16 to 1024 px;
- maskable icons, Apple touch icon and favicon;
- token files, guidelines, quick guides and provenance;
- product-application, map, icon, motion, pattern and photography specifications.

## Color governance

Brand color is separated into three layers:

1. `platform.*` — shared identity structure;
2. `destination.*` — local territorial accents;
3. `semantic.*` — product meaning such as success, warning, error and information.

Destination accents never redefine semantic status colors.

Coral `#FA7951` is the shared human/opportunity/value node, not a universal literal sun.

## Validation boundary

No claim of real community validation is made. Until research with residents, businesses and local cultural/tourism stakeholders is conducted, destination validation remains `INTERNAL`.

Itacaré tagline selection remains `NEEDS_HUMAN_VALIDATION`.

## Supersession

Historical V1 assets remain preserved. Supersession is explicit and never deletes provenance. Runtime replacement of product assets is a separate bounded ChangeSet and requires visual-regression/accessibility proof before release.
