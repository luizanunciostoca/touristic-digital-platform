- `GAP` — no V2 Business-owned equivalent exists yet.

M59 promotes `Profile sandbox/runtime` from `GAP` to `PASS`. The frozen V1 module is confirmed as an active onboarding dependency, but its mixed partner-workspace and commercial-conversion imports are not treated as justification to collapse those boundaries into the profile milestone.

V2 now builds the tutorial profile through the existing `normalizeBusinessProfile()` core, preserving sanitized name, category, specialty, location, CTA and optional demonstration promotion. The `profile` onboarding step lazily loads the dedicated subpath, persists `tutorialBusinessProfile`, renders the preview in the existing onboarding surface and exposes map, primary and promotion actions as tutorial-only events. Rating and opening-hours state are not fabricated, and all tutorial profile activity is marked `excludeFromBusinessMetrics: true`.

Executable M59 validation exposed and corrected an explicit runtime-context allowlist omission. `tutorialBusinessProfile` was initially discarded by `updateBusinessOnboardingRuntimeContext()`, and the same audit found that the M58 keys `tutorialBusinessCandidate` and `businessRecommendationResult` were also absent. The allowlist now adds only these known tutorial result keys. A regression test proves they persist while an unrelated credential-like field remains rejected.

`Business profile behavior` remains `PARTIAL`: M59 closes the tutorial sandbox/runtime contract but does not claim wider production persistence/profile breadth. `Live Business runtime` also remains `PARTIAL` because partner workspace, analytics and other frozen runtime breadth are still incomplete.

The M54 runtime-isolation rule remains intact. Business onboarding consumes Search, location, Assistant and Navigation through explicit ports/subpaths; profile projection reuses the Business core; Auth remains the authorization authority; Payments remains separate.

## Dependency rule

No Business implementation milestone may introduce its own credential/session/cookie/CSRF logic. Those contracts belong to `FEATURE-0008` and must be consumed through an Auth-owned boundary.

The onboarding workflow session remains product/workflow state only and is not an authentication authority. Its runtime allowlist persists only explicit product/tutorial keys and rejects unrelated fields.

Payment/checkout execution remains owned by `FEATURE-0009`; onboarding may expose a future commercial handoff but must not absorb Payments ownership.
