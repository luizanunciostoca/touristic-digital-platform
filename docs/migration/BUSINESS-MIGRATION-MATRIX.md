## M61 score

- `PASS`: 16
- `PARTIAL`: 3
- `GAP`: 0
- `N/A`: 1
- total: 20

M61 removes the final Business-owned `GAP` without implementing Payments. The frozen V1 audit showed that `business-commercial-conversion-adapter.js` owned plan recommendation, contractor collection, consent capture and construction of the payment request, while `business-checkout-client.js` owned POSTing checkout sessions, idempotency headers, opening the provider URL, polling status and emitting a verified payment result.

`@touristic/business/onboarding-commercial-conversion` preserves only the Business half of that seam. Its checkout value is explicitly marked `requiresPaymentsCapability: true` and contains no provider URL, token, checkout identifier, polling state, payment status or network behavior. The package can only transform a separately verified same-session result into the Business activation state `READY_TO_CONVERT`.

The executable unit contract proves the frozen objective-to-plan mapping, sanitization and mandatory contractor fields, versioned required terms/privacy with optional marketing consent, absence of Payments execution fields from the handoff and rejection of unverified or cross-session payment results.

`Business profile behavior` and `Live Business runtime` remain `PARTIAL`; M61 does not invent missing production persistence, analytics endpoints or a live checkout UI. `FEATURE-0005` therefore remains `baseline-pending` until the remaining partial production breadth is proven independently.

## Dependency rule

No Business implementation milestone may introduce its own credential/session/cookie/CSRF logic. Those contracts belong to `FEATURE-0008` and must be consumed through an Auth-owned boundary.

Payment/checkout execution remains owned by `FEATURE-0009`. Business may prepare an immutable commercial request and consume a verified result, but must not create checkout sessions, manage provider redirects/tokens, poll payment status or act as the authority that verifies a transaction.
