M61 promotes `Commercial conversion adapter` from `GAP` to `PASS`. The frozen V1 audit separates Business-owned commercial preparation from Payments-owned execution: Business recommends a plan, collects contractor data and required legal acceptances, then hands a normalized payload to checkout. Payment is never considered confirmed from the request itself.

`@touristic/business/onboarding-commercial` owns the immutable plan vocabulary, objective-based recommendation, contractor sanitization, versioned Terms/Privacy acceptance, optional marketing consent, checkout-handoff construction and same-correlation verification gate. The onboarding runtime persists only explicit M61 keys and dispatches `businessCheckoutRequested` with `requiresPaymentProvider: true`.

The `finish` step renders the commercial preparation form and states that no payment is executed there. The handoff correlation derives from the onboarding workflow creation time and is not an Auth credential or proof of payment. A future Payments-owned implementation must execute checkout and emit a trustworthy verification signal. Wrong-session or unverified signals are rejected; only a verified same-correlation signal produces `businessCommercialActivationReady`.

The permanent Commercial Browser Contract proves the full boundary in Chromium: `events` recommends `performance`, contractor input is sanitized, required consents are persisted, the checkout request contains no synthetic payment success, wrong/unverified confirmations fail closed and exactly one verified same-correlation confirmation is accepted.

`Business profile behavior`, `Live Business runtime` and broader `Business domain/browser behavior` remain `PARTIAL`. `Checkout client` remains `N/A` for Business because provider/payment execution belongs to `FEATURE-0009`. M61 therefore reaches zero Business-owned GAPs without claiming full Business equivalence.

## Dependency rule

No Business implementation milestone may introduce its own credential/session/cookie/CSRF logic. Those contracts belong to `FEATURE-0008` and must be consumed through an Auth-owned boundary.

The onboarding workflow session remains product/workflow state only and is not an authentication authority. Its runtime allowlist persists only explicit product/tutorial keys and rejects unrelated fields.

Payment/checkout execution remains owned by `FEATURE-0009`; onboarding may expose a future commercial handoff but must not absorb Payments ownership.
